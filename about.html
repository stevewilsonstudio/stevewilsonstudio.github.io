About

I’ve been drawing and painting since a very young age, and art has long been my way of observing and engaging with the world. My work is grounded in close observation and a sustained interest in representational painting shaped by light, atmosphere, and place.

At sixteen, I studied in the studio atelier of sculptor and painter Michael Skop, an experience that emphasized working directly from life and developing disciplined habits of looking. More recently, I’ve continued my studies with Angela Campbell, OPA, with a focus on technical execution and the professionalism needed for a consistent studio practice.

My paintings grow out of time spent in nature and in observation of life.
