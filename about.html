about

Steve Wilson has been drawing and painting since a very young age, and art has long been his way of observing and engaging with the world. His work is grounded in close observation and a sustained interest in representational painting shaped by light, atmosphere, and place.

At sixteen, he studied in the studio atelier of sculptor and painter Michael Skop, an experience that emphasized working directly from life and developing disciplined habits of observation. Skop’s approach—rooted in an apprenticeship tradition and a phenomenological way of seeing—had a lasting influence on how he understands perception and form. More recently, he has continued his studies with Angela Campbell, OPA, where the focus has been on refining technical execution and developing the consistency and professionalism required for a sustained studio practice.

His paintings grow out of time spent in nature and in the quiet observation of everyday life.
